**Camera: DNG writer fallback for missing CalibrationIlluminant1 and portrait status pill (Android, 2026-11-27)**

- Fixed capture failures (`Missing metadata fields for tag CalibrationIlluminant1 (c65a)`) on cameras whose HAL omits `SENSOR_REFERENCE_ILLUMINANT1` (such as the OnePlus 11 front camera): `CameraController` now falls back from `DngCreator` to `MaxResDngWriter.writeBuffer`, which defaults a missing `CalibrationIlluminant1` to D65 (and falls back to an sRGB D65 matrix if both color matrices are absent) across single-frame, burst, ZSL burst, and long-exposure captures.
- Fixed the portrait top bar collapsing the viewfinder when localized status strings (such as Ukrainian "Об'єднання кадрів…") wrapped into a single-character vertical column beside the toolbar buttons: portrait capture/merging/saving/recording status now renders as a single-line translucent pill overlaid at the top center of the viewfinder.
- Code: `app-camera/src/main/java/app/rawboy/camera/capture/MaxResDngWriter.kt`, `app-camera/src/main/java/app/rawboy/camera/capture/CameraController.kt`, `app-camera/src/main/java/app/rawboy/camera/ui/CameraScreen.kt`; Tests: `app-camera/src/test/java/app/rawboy/camera/capture/MaxResDngWriterTest.kt`.
