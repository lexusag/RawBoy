**Camera: Exposure works like P mode (Android, 2026-11-28)**

- **Brightness is now Exposure**: −3 to +3 EV in thirds of a stop, shown as a camera shows it (+⅔, −1⅓). Landscape's **B** chip is now **E**. Your old Brightness becomes the nearest third.
- The camera always meters the scene at 0 and the viewfinder shows the change at once, with no drift while the camera's auto exposure catches up.
- Photos at a setting other than 0 are exposed by hand along a program line: brighter = longer shutter up to 1/30 s, then higher ISO, then a longer shutter up to the still limit; darker = lowest ISO, then a faster shutter. Shutter times stay flicker-safe under mains light. What the sensor can't give is added in the develop, so the photo matches the viewfinder. Merged bursts are exposed the same way.
- Ring-buffer (ZSL) frames, flash, long exposures, Street mode and video keep the metered exposure and get Exposure in the develop.
- New **Settings › Shutter speed and ISO** (off by default): the shutter speed and ISO the next photo will get, in a corner of the viewfinder. Street mode always shows it.
