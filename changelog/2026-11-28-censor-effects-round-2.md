**Censor: new effects and a smooth edge (Web, 2026-11-28)**

- Paint is now **Oil paint**: two coats of fine oil strokes that follow the area's shapes (default size 1.1%).
- **Mosaic** gets **Glass colour**: the pieces turn into tinted coloured glass (default size 1.3%).
- New **Halftone** (coarse CMYK dots, Contrast), **Hatching** (pen lines, Contrast, Keep colour) and **Ink brush** (dry black strokes, or the brush colour).
- Ink and Heal away are gone: old edits open with Frosted glass and Oil paint in their place.
- Edge is gone: every stroke fades out smoothly over the brush's Hardness (50 by default).
