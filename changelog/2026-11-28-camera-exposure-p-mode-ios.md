**Camera: Exposure works like P mode (iOS, 2026-11-28)**

- **Brightness is now Exposure** on the iPhone too: −3 to +3 EV in thirds of a stop (+⅔, −1⅓). Landscape's **B** chip is now **E**. Your old Brightness becomes the nearest third.
- The camera meters the scene at 0 and the viewfinder shows the change at once.
- Photos at a setting other than 0 are exposed by hand along the same program line as on Android (longer shutter up to 1/30 s, then higher ISO, then up to 1/15 s; darker = lowest ISO, then a faster shutter; flicker-safe). What the sensor can't give is added in the develop. Flash photos keep the iPhone's flash exposure and get Exposure in the develop.
- New **Settings › Shutter speed and ISO** (off by default): the shutter speed and ISO the next photo will get, top right of the viewfinder.
