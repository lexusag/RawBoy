RawBoy Web: raw photo editor in your browser

RawBoy develops RAW photos with an AgX tone mapper, film looks and real-time sliders, right in the browser. The decoder (LibRaw and the Adobe DNG SDK) runs locally as WebAssembly and the develop shader runs on your GPU with WebGL2, so photos are never uploaded.

Three tools in one page

  • RawBoy: every control. Exposure, white balance, AgX, filmic or sigmoid tone mapping, curves, colour grading, LUTs, lens corrections, masks, denoise, grain, halation, bloom and light leaks.
  • RawBoy Auto: develops a whole batch automatically into a film look, with presets and one-click export of every photo.
  • RawBoy Browser: culls a folder of raws from their embedded previews, with stars, picks and rejects written to XMP sidecars that Lightroom, Capture One and darktable read.

Formats

Opens Canon CR2 and CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF, Panasonic RW2, Olympus ORF, Pentax PEF and DNG, including DNG 1.7 with JPEG XL, plus JPEG, PNG and TIFF (HEIC in Safari). Exports JPEG (jpegli, 4:4:4) or 8/16-bit TIFF.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer; needs WebGL2. RawBoy is also an Android app; source and releases are on GitHub. Privacy policy.
