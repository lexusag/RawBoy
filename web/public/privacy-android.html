Privacy policy

RawBoy, RawBoy Auto and RawBoy Camera for Android · Last updated 23 November 2026

The RawBoy apps are free photo apps made by an independent developer, Oleksii Mitskevych, not a company. They have no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics of their own. This page explains what happens to your photos and data.

Your photos stay on your phone

  • Photos you open are decoded and developed on your phone, by the app itself. They are never uploaded to RawBoy or to any server: RawBoy runs no server.
  • Exports and photos you take are saved in your phone's Pictures folder (for example Pictures/RawBoy), where only you and the apps you allow can see them.
  • Faces and skin are found on the phone (for exposure, skin retouch and blemish removal) with Google's MediaPipe library. The photo never leaves the phone for this.
  • Depth maps (RawBoy Camera, only if you turn on the experimental Save depth map) are made on the phone: from the camera's own depth or focus pixels, or estimated from the photo by an AI model that ships inside the app (Depth Anything V2). The map is saved inside the photo's own file. Nothing is uploaded.

Permissions

  • Camera (RawBoy Camera): to show the viewfinder and take photos.
  • Location (RawBoy Camera, only if you turn on Geotag): to write where a photo was taken into that photo's own file. It is asked for only when you turn Geotag on, used only while the camera is on screen, and never sent anywhere by the app.
  • RawBoy and RawBoy Auto ask for no permissions: you choose each photo with Android's own file picker or "Open with".

What is stored on your phone

Your settings, presets, the batch you are working on, lens presets and RawBoy Camera's learned camera calibration (including the dual-pixel distance calibration) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android's settings.

Usage statistics sent by Google's MediaPipe library

MediaPipe, the Google library that finds faces and skin on the phone, may send anonymous technical statistics to Google: which of its features ran, how long it took, errors, the app's name and version, the phone model and the Android version. It sends no photos, no images, no location and nothing that identifies you. RawBoy does not receive this data. Google handles it under Google's privacy policy.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

RawBoy Camera runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. RawBoy Camera uses it only while you run the experimental Calibrate dual-pixel distances in Settings: ARCore measures distances from the camera's picture on the phone, and RawBoy keeps only the few calibration numbers it works out, in the app's private storage. RawBoy sends nothing to Google for this and does not receive any data from Google. Google Play Services for AR is a separate app from Google; how it handles data is described in Google's privacy policy. If it isn't installed, RawBoy Camera offers to install it from Google Play, and only when you start the calibration.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no personal information from anyone.
