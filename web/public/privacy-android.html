Privacy policy

RawBoy, RawBoy Auto, RawBoy Camera and RawBoy Film for Android · Last updated 31 October 2026

The RawBoy apps are free photo apps made by an independent developer, Oleksii Mitskevych, not a company. They have no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics of their own. This page explains what happens to your photos and data.

Your photos stay on your phone

  • Photos you open are decoded and developed on your phone, by the app itself. They are never uploaded to RawBoy or to any server: RawBoy runs no server.
  • Exports and photos you take are saved in your phone's Pictures folder (for example Pictures/RawBoy), where only you and the apps you allow can see them.
  • Faces and skin are found on the phone (for exposure, skin retouch and blemish removal) with Google's MediaPipe library. The photo never leaves the phone for this.

Permissions

  • Camera (RawBoy Camera and RawBoy Film): to show the viewfinder and take photos.
  • Location (RawBoy Camera and RawBoy Film, only if you turn on Geotag): to write where a photo was taken into that photo's own file. It is asked for only when you turn Geotag on, used only while the camera is on screen, and never sent anywhere by the app.
  • RawBoy and RawBoy Auto ask for no permissions: you choose each photo with Android's own file picker or "Open with".

What is stored on your phone

Your settings, presets, the batch you are working on and lens presets are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android's settings.

Usage statistics sent by Google's MediaPipe library

MediaPipe, the Google library that finds faces and skin on the phone, may send anonymous technical statistics to Google: which of its features ran, how long it took, errors, the app's name and version, the phone model and the Android version. It sends no photos, no images, no location and nothing that identifies you. RawBoy does not receive this data. Google handles it under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: lexusag@gmail.com or an issue at github.com/lexusag/RawBoy/issues.
