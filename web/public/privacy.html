Privacy policy

RawBoy Web (app.rawboy.link) · Last updated 26 October 2026

RawBoy Web is a free raw photo editor that runs in your browser. It is made by an independent developer, not a company, and has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains what happens to your photos and data.

Your photos stay in your browser

  • Photos you open (from your device or from Google Drive) are decoded and developed inside your browser, with WebAssembly and your GPU. They are never uploaded to RawBoy or to any server run by RawBoy. There is no such server: the site is only static files.
  • Exports are saved where you choose: a download, a folder on your device, your photo library (iPhone and iPad), or a Google Drive folder you pick.

What is stored on your device

To remember your settings between visits, RawBoy keeps a few things in your browser's own storage (local storage, IndexedDB and the origin private file system) on your device only:

  • preferences such as the last tab, export format and sort order, and your presets;
  • access to folders you opened, so they can be reopened (only if you allow it in the browser);
  • a temporary cache of photo previews, emptied when the app starts again.

None of this is sent anywhere. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. RawBoy sets no cookies.

Google Drive

If you choose From Google Drive or Save to: Google Drive, and only then:

  • You sign in with Google in a window run by Google, and grant RawBoy the drive.file permission. With it, RawBoy can only see and change the files you pick in Google's file picker and the files RawBoy itself saves to your Drive. It cannot list, read or change anything else in your Drive.
  • Files you pick are downloaded straight from Google into your browser to be developed. Exports you save to Drive are uploaded straight from your browser to the folder you pick. Nothing passes through any other server.
  • RawBoy uses this access only to open the photos you pick and to save your exports. It does not store your Google data on any server, does not share it with anyone, does not use it for advertising and does not use it to train AI or machine learning models.
  • The sign-in token Google gives RawBoy is kept only in the page's memory and expires after an hour; it is gone when you close or reload the page. You can remove RawBoy's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

RawBoy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. RawBoy does not receive or use this data. When you use Google Drive, Google's privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/lexusag/RawBoy/issues.
