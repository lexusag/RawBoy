RawBoy Web

RawBoy Web is a free photo editor for photographers. It develops the RAW files that digital cameras record (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Olympus, Pentax, DNG) into finished JPEG or TIFF photos, in the same way as Lightroom or darktable: exposure, white balance, contrast, colour, curves, lens corrections, noise reduction, and film looks. JPEG, PNG, TIFF and HEIC photos work too. Everything runs inside your browser on your own computer or phone.

What it does

  • RawBoy: develop one photo with every control (tone mapping, white balance, colour, curves, LUTs, masks, crop and rotate, grain, light leaks).
  • RawBoy Auto: develop a whole batch of photos automatically into a film look, then export them all.
  • RawBoy Browser: go through a folder of raws, rate them with stars and pick or reject them.

How it uses Google Drive

Google Drive is optional. Photographers who keep their photos in Drive can use it instead of local files:

  • Open from Google Drive: you pick photos in Google's own file picker; RawBoy downloads only those files into your browser and develops them there.
  • Save to Google Drive: you pick a Drive folder and RawBoy uploads the photos you export into it.

RawBoy asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can reach only the files you pick and the files it saves, nothing else in your Drive. Your Google data never goes to a RawBoy server (there is none), is not shared, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Details are in the privacy policy.

No generative AI

RawBoy is a conventional photo developer. It does not generate, synthesise or alter people's likeness, and it cannot create AI-generated imagery of any kind, including intimate or sexual imagery. It only adjusts the tones, colours and detail of photos you took. The one machine-learning feature, skin retouching, uses Google's MediaPipe models on your device to find skin so it can be smoothed slightly; nothing is generated and nothing is sent anywhere.

Who makes it

RawBoy is made by an independent photographer and developer. It is also an Android app, and its source and releases are on GitHub. Questions: GitHub issues.
